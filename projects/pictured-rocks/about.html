About Pictured Rocks Michigan

Pictured Rocks National Lakeshore is a stunning natural wonder located along Lake Superior in the Upper Peninsula of Michigan. The park's name comes from its colorful cliffs, made of sandstone and shaped by wind, water, and time. The Grand Sable Dunes, stretching five miles along the coast, offer stunning views of the surrounding area. Visitors can hike up the dunes or take a scenic drive along the shoreline. The park also features numerous waterfalls, including Miners Falls, Spray Falls, and Sable Falls, providing a serene atmosphere for relaxation and reflection. The park also boasts some of the most pristine beaches in the Midwest, including Sand Point Beach and Miners Beach, perfect for swimming, sunbathing, and picnicking. The crystal-clear waters of Lake Superior provide a refreshing respite from the summer heat, and visitors can explore the underwater world through snorkeling or kayaking. In winter, the park transforms into a snowy wonderland, offering cross-country skiing, snowshoeing, and ice climbing. The park is also home to a diverse array of wildlife, including bald eagles, peregrine falcons, piping plover, black bears, white-tailed deer, and red foxes.
